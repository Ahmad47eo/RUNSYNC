Safe next steps

  1. Check the treadmill's exact model number on its rating label or manual while the treadmill is stopped.
  2. Use FitShow's own device scan and normal workout screen to confirm the treadmill is recognized; do not pair it through iPhone Bluetooth settings if FitShow's instructions say to pair inside the app.
  3. Ask FitShow support or the treadmill seller whether this exact model supports the official FitShow SDK or standard FTMS, and request written documentation.
  4. Until confirmed, RUNSYNC stays read-only and disconnected: no scanning, pairing, speed commands, or Bluetooth writes.
